Background Remover

Choose an image

Best for solid or plain backgrounds. JPG, PNG and WebP are supported by most browsers.

This local tool removes edge-connected pixels close to the colours found in the image corners. It is not an AI model; complex scenes may leave some background.

Raise the value for uneven or shadowed backgrounds; lower it to protect similar-coloured edges.

Download PNG

Remove a background and keep the subject

ToolsTap Background Remover detects colours close to the image corners and removes matching pixels that connect to an outer edge. This edge-aware approach is useful for product photos, icons, simple logos and other images photographed against a single-colour backdrop.

How to get cleaner cut-outs

Start with a sharp source image. Move the tolerance a little at a time, and inspect light hair, white clothing and soft shadows before downloading. For complex scenes with overlapping colours, a dedicated AI segmentation model or manual masking will usually produce better edges.
