What Is an Indirect Tax Calculator?

An indirect-tax calculator helps you add a tax to a net price or extract the tax from a tax-inclusive (gross) price. Different countries use different names for this kind of tax — GST (Goods and Services Tax), VAT (Value Added Tax), Sales Tax, and other local terms — but the underlying math is the same.

Two Directions of Calculation

  • Add tax (tax-exclusive): you have a net amount and want to know the tax and the total.
    Tax = Base × Rate ÷ 100
    Total = Base + Tax
  • Remove tax (tax-inclusive): you have a gross amount and want to know the base and the tax.
    Base = Gross ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)
    Tax = Gross − Base

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select the country or region. The tax name (GST, VAT, Sales Tax, etc.) updates automatically.
  2. Select a currency for display. Currency does not affect the math.
  3. Choose Add Tax or Remove Tax depending on what you want to compute.
  4. Enter the amount and the tax rate. A reference default rate is pre-filled for the selected country, but you can override it.
  5. Click Calculate Tax to see the base amount, tax amount, and total.

Important Note About Rates

Indirect-tax rates change over time and vary by jurisdiction, product or service category, and transaction type. Preset rates shown here are reference defaults only and may not reflect the current legal rate. Always verify the correct rate with the relevant tax authority or a qualified professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GST / VAT / sales tax calculator?

It is a tool that helps you add an indirect tax to a net amount, or extract the tax from a tax-inclusive (gross) amount. Different countries use different names such as GST, VAT, or Sales Tax, but the underlying math is the same.

How do I add tax to a price?

If you have a net (tax-exclusive) amount and want to add tax, use the Tax Exclusive mode. The tax equals the net amount multiplied by the rate divided by 100, and the total equals the net amount plus the tax.

How do I remove tax from a tax-inclusive price?

If you have a gross (tax-inclusive) amount and want to find the base amount, use the Tax Inclusive mode. The base equals the gross amount divided by (1 + rate/100), and the tax equals the gross amount minus the base.

Why do tax rates vary by country or region?

Indirect tax rates are set by each jurisdiction and often depend on the type of goods or services, the region, and current legislation. Our preset rates are only reference defaults and may not reflect the current legal rate.

Is this calculator suitable for official tax filing?

No. This calculator is for estimation and educational purposes only. For official tax filing, always use the current official rates and consult a qualified accountant or the relevant tax authority.
