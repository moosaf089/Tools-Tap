How to Use the Age Calculator

Our free online age calculator makes it easy to find out exactly how old you are. Whether you need to calculate your age in years, months, and days, or want to know your age in total weeks, hours, or even seconds, this tool gives you accurate results instantly.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Enter your date of birth using the three dropdown menus (month, day, and year).
  2. Select the target date (defaults to today) to calculate your age at that specific date.
  3. Click "Calculate Age" to see your exact age displayed in multiple units.

How the Age Is Calculated

We count your complete calendar years first, then the remaining complete months, then the remaining days. When your birth day does not exist in a given month/year (for example, February 29 in a non-leap year), the anniversary is treated as the last valid day of that month. This is the standard convention used on official forms, and it means the year, month, and day counts always add up consistently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator work?

The age calculator determines your exact age by comparing your date of birth with a target date (usually today's date). It calculates the difference in complete calendar years, months, and days, and also provides your total age in weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

Is this age calculator free to use?

Yes, our age calculator is completely free to use. There are no hidden fees, no registration required, and no limits on how many times you can use it.

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes. You can calculate your age at any future date by changing the "Age at the Date of" field. This is useful for planning events, calculating age at retirement, or determining how old you will be on a specific date.

Does the age calculator account for leap years?

Yes, our age calculator correctly handles leap years and varying month lengths. When a birth day does not exist in a given year (for example, February 29 in a non-leap year), the tool uses the last valid day of that month as the anniversary, which is the standard calendar-age convention.

Can I calculate age in months only?

Yes, the calculator shows your age in total months, total weeks, total days, total hours, total minutes, and total seconds, in addition to the standard years, months, and days format.
