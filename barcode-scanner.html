Scan a barcode from a photo or camera with ToolsTap

A barcode scanner reads the encoded bars or symbols in an image and returns the value stored in that code. A decoded number is not automatically a product identity, verified price or inventory record; those require a separate product database.

Practical tips for better results

Use bright, even lighting, keep the barcode in focus and avoid glare. ToolsTap Barcode Scanner decodes supported barcodes in the browser when camera access or an image is available.

ToolsTap provides this free online tool with an emphasis on clear controls and practical results. Always review the output before sharing, publishing or using it for an important decision.
