How the Currency Converter Works

This tool converts an amount from one currency into another using the latest available exchange rate from our data source. It does not use a real-time interbank feed, and it does not include bank margins or fees.

Step-by-Step

  1. Enter the amount you have.
  2. Select the currency you have (FROM).
  3. Select the currency you want (TO).
  4. Click Convert Currency. The tool fetches the latest available rate and converts immediately.

Rate Source and Freshness

Rates are provided by the Frankfurter API, which publishes European Central Bank (ECB) reference rates. ECB rates are typically updated once per working day. The tool caches the last successful fetch for about 15 minutes and refreshes automatically. The exact rate date returned by the API is displayed on the result card.

Important Disclaimer

The rates shown here are reference rates and may differ from what your bank, card network, or currency exchange actually charges. This tool is for informational and estimation purposes only and is not financial advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the currency converter work?

You choose the currency you have (FROM), enter the amount, choose the currency you want (TO), and click Convert. The tool fetches the latest available exchange rate from our data source and multiplies your amount by that rate to give the converted value.

What is an exchange rate?

An exchange rate is the price of one currency expressed in another currency. For example, if 1 USD = 278 PKR, then 278 is the exchange rate of USD to PKR.

How does USD to PKR conversion work?

To convert USD to PKR, the tool multiplies the amount in USD by the current USD/PKR rate. For example, 100 USD × 278 PKR/USD = 27,800 PKR.

Are the rates live or current?

Rates are the latest available reference rates from our data source. They are updated periodically, not tick-by-tick. The rate date is always shown on the result card.

Why can the rate differ from my bank or card provider?

Banks and card providers add margins, fees, and use their own rate feeds. The rate shown here is a reference rate and will usually differ from what your bank or card network charges you.

How often are the displayed rates updated?

The data source publishes reference rates periodically. Our converter caches the last successful fetch and refreshes automatically. The exact rate date is shown on the result card.
