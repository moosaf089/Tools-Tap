What is an Image Converter?

An image converter is a software tool that changes a digital image's file format (e.g., from JPG to PNG) without significantly altering its visual content. Our free online image converter works directly in your browser, allowing you to convert JPG, PNG, and WebP files quickly and securely. Whether you need to make a JPEG transparent by converting it to PNG or shrink a photo for web use by converting it to WebP, this tool has you covered.

How to Convert an Image

  1. Upload Your Image: Drag and drop a file onto the upload area or click "Browse Files" to select an image from your device. We support JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP inputs.
  2. Choose Your Format: Click on one of the format options (JPG, PNG, or WebP) to select your desired output format.
  3. Convert & Download: Click the "Convert" button. Once the process is complete, the "Download" button will activate, allowing you to save your newly converted image.

Supported Conversion Paths

This tool is designed for converting common web image formats. Supported outputs are:

  • Convert to JPG: JPG (or JPEG) is a lossy format ideal for photographs. Converting to JPG is great for reducing file size for web use. Note that JPG does not support transparency.
  • Convert to PNG: PNG is a lossless format that supports transparency, making it perfect for logos, icons, and graphics with text.
  • Convert to WebP: WebP is a modern format developed by Google that offers superior lossy and lossless compression. It's the recommended format for modern websites, providing smaller file sizes with comparable quality.
Why can't I convert to GIF, BMP, or ICO?
Browsers' built-in image encoders do not natively support creating true GIF, BMP, or ICO files from a canvas. Implementing a reliable encoder would require adding heavy, third-party JavaScript libraries, which conflicts with our goal of keeping the tool lightweight and fast. We prioritize honest, high-quality conversions over providing a feature that would produce corrupted or fake files.
Is this tool safe and private to use?
Absolutely. This image converter is 100% client-side. This means all processing happens directly in your web browser on your own computer. Your image files are never uploaded to our server, ensuring complete privacy and security. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded and the tool will still work.
What happens to the background when converting to JPG?
The JPG format does not support transparency. If you convert an image with a transparent background (like a PNG) to JPG, this tool will automatically fill the transparent areas with a solid white color. This ensures the output is a standard, clean-looking JPG file without unexpected artifacts.

Privacy and Security

Your privacy is our priority. The ToolsTap Image Converter processes all files locally in your browser. No data is sent to or stored on our servers. This guarantees your images remain confidential and secure.
