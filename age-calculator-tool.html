Calculate age accurately from a date of birth with ToolsTap

An age calculator compares a date of birth with a selected reference date and returns the difference in calendar years, months and days. Use the result for personal records, forms and planning, and confirm any age rule that depends on a local law or organisation.

Practical tips for better results

For a reliable result, check the day, month and year carefully and remember that leap years affect day totals. ToolsTap Age Calculator runs the date calculation in your browser; it is a convenience tool, not a source of legal eligibility advice.
