What to expect when converting PDF to Word with ToolsTap

A PDF is designed to preserve page appearance, while a Word document is designed for editing and reflow. When a conversion extracts selectable text, multi-column layouts, images, tables and scanned text may not translate exactly.

Practical tips for better results

Review headings, paragraphs, symbols and page breaks in Word. Scanned pages require OCR before text can be extracted. ToolsTap PDF to Word focuses on text conversion and should not be treated as a pixel-perfect reconstruction of every source PDF.
